About me

Aleks Stepančič

I’m a master’s student in Data Science at ETH Zurich, interested in optimization, reinforcement learning, world models, and stochastic processes.

My studies at ETH are supported by an Ad Futura Scholarship. I previously studied Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Ljubljana, where I graduated among the top three in my cohort and received the Dean’s Prize. My thesis explored integer partition identities.

In 2024, I worked as a C++ developer for Stan, implementing rank-normalized R-hat for HMC convergence diagnostics.

Alongside my studies, I’ve worked on LLM hallucinations at the ETH AI Center, Quine–McCluskey optimization using SIMD register vectorization, and illicit Bitcoin transaction detection with graph neural networks.

If you’d like to see more of what I’ve studied, I’ve put together a list of my courses.

Away from the keyboard, I play competitive table tennis, chess, and poker.

I’m based in Zurich, Switzerland. You can email me and find me on LinkedIn.
